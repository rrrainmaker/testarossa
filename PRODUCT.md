# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Pinned by the user: "next js tailwind, etc. … like standard stack, after you done deploy to cloudfare". Next.js 16 (App Router) as a static export (`output: "export"`), Tailwind CSS v4, TypeScript, fonts self-hosted via `next/font`, no animation libraries. Deployed to Cloudflare Pages with `npm run deploy` (wrangler).

## Users
Inferred: the user and whoever they show it to. It is a personal showcase of a scroll-driven motion piece, viewed on a laptop trackpad first and on phones second.

## Product Purpose
A one-page, scroll-scrubbed motion story about the 1984 Testarossa. It recreates the choreography of a fan-made "Tesla Roadster concept site" shown in a YouTube short (https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ykYAHJeZObU), retold for a different car. Success means the motion reads as one continuous, authored sequence, as cool as the reference.

## Positioning
A fan concept, not a dealer or brand page. Its distinctive mechanism is continuity: every scene grows out of the previous one. A pencil line becomes the car, the red ticker becomes a red wipe, the wipe becomes the T of the name, and the name disappears in tyre smoke.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Scroll-driven. Must work on desktop trackpads and on touch phones, and honour `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- No commerce. There is nothing to buy or reserve.
- Must not use Ferrari logos, the prancing horse, or Ferrari's lettering. All car art is original vector illustration.
- Tesla code and assets are not used. Only the choreography idea from the short is borrowed.

## Brand Commitments
Brief-pinned: "make it like that … but for a Ferrari Testarossa". That means the reference's white ground, red band and red wordmark grammar, retold with the Testarossa's own facts and era.

## Evidence on Hand
Only public specification facts for European-market cars: 4,943 cc flat-twelve, 48 valves, 390 CV at 6,300 rpm, 490 Nm at 4,500 rpm, 0–100 km/h in 5.8 s, 290 km/h, 1,506 kg dry, 4,485 / 1,976 / 1,130 mm (length / width / height), 2,550 mm wheelbase, built 1984–1991 (7,177 cars), five-speed manual with rear-wheel drive, a mid-mounted engine behind the seats, tail-lamps behind a louvred full-width grille, side radiators fed by the strakes, and the name meaning "red head" after the red cam covers. No photography is used, and no claims beyond these.

## Product Principles
1. Continuity over sections: each scene is born from the last.
2. The car is the hero. Type and colour stage it and never compete with it.
3. Only true facts about the car, labelled as a fan concept.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-reachable navigation, a skip link to the spec sheet, real text for every claim (the ticker is decorative), and a reduced-motion path.
